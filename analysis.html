Nucor Steel
2022
Strategies

TalentLMSExcelPowerPointZoom
Safety Training Needs Analysis — Compliance & Engagement Audit
The Challenge
To ensure compliance and engagement in safety trainings, Nucor Steel requested a review of an existing training plan to highlight gaps in learning, management needs, and overall learner engagement.
The Solution
Review of TalentLMS course data including completion rates, time-in-training, and department engagement. User surveys and a one-hour facilitated feedback session with safety representatives from each department. Recommendations covered content updates, role-relevant course creation, and engagement strategies.
Responsibilities
  • →Curated user data from LMS and survey responses
  • →Developed PowerPoint stakeholder summary covering statistics, login trends, top completions, and cost savings ($906K estimated value)
  • →Facilitated department safety rep feedback session
  • →Created prioritized plan for course updates
Key Data Point: LMS showed 10,688 logins at peak (2021) declining to 7,514 through September 2022 — combined with survey feedback, this surfaced a clear need for content refresh and manager accountability strategies.

Cohort 2 Curriculum Redesign — DevOps Bootcamp LMS Migration
The Challenge
After a successful first cohort, Kura Labs needed to scale their second cohort with asynchronous learning support via SAP Litmos while updating the curriculum to match the current technology landscape.
The Solution
Deep-dive into first cohort outcomes produced two objectives: (1) update the curriculum with hands-on labs matching technology growth, and (2) migrate into SAP Litmos to provide learner transparency and a grade database. A Draw.io content map organized content by competency with assessments.
Responsibilities
  • →Reviewed existing first-cohort curriculum end-to-end
  • →Interviewed CEO and course instructor to develop new objectives and hands-on lab experiences
  • →Built complete course content in SAP Litmos LMS
  • →Developed student pathways and grade reports
  • →Created partial Draw.io course content map grouped by competency and assessment
Deliverable Highlight: Content map organized AWS/DevOps competencies (monitoring, networking, infrastructure-as-code) into a sequenced diagram with activities, assessments, and Flask App deployments as capstone checkpoints.

B-Corp eLearning Certification — Asynchronous Course Development
The Challenge
Inspiring Capital wanted to develop eLearning certification for individuals and small businesses interested in B-Corp status, expanding their consulting services with a scalable online offering.
The Solution
Review of existing content assets (podcasts, written materials, recorded ILT sessions) and development of learning objectives for an asynchronous solution. Applied adult learning theory and chunking best practices. Course built and deployed in Thinkific.
Responsibilities
  • →Reviewed all existing content assets including podcasts and ILT recordings
  • →Interviewed CEO to surface learning objectives aligned with business goals
  • →Generated course outline with recommended media formats per module
  • →Edited course content and built the complete course in Thinkific
Approach: Applied adult learning theory to determine appropriate chunking, sequencing, and media pairing — matching existing ILT assets to asynchronous formats (text, video, quiz, discussion) without losing engagement or depth.
